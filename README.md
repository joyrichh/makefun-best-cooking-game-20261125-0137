**Kuali Kilat — Midnight at the Warung** is a cozy, quick-footed cooking game about keeping one tiny late-night kitchen running. Fetch ingredients, prep the right recipe, keep an eye on the wok, and send each meal to a matching order ticket before the guests run out of patience.

**How to play**
- Walk with `WASD` or the arrow keys; click a destination to walk there.
- Stand by a crate and press `E` or `Space` to pick up its ingredient. Click a crate to walk over and pick it up.
- At the chopping board, press and hold `E` or `Space` until chopping finishes. At the wok, place a cook-ready ingredient and come back after the timer finishes; leaving it too long burns it.
- Bring fully prepped ingredients to the plating counter, add every item on an open ticket, pick up the finished plate, and serve it at the right-side window.
- Take one wrong or burnt ingredient at a time to the trash. Consecutive, timely deliveries earn streak multipliers and tips; the last thirty seconds bring doubled tips and the rush.
- Press `P` or `Esc` to pause, `M` to toggle sound, and `R` or `Enter` to restart after the shift.

**How to run**

Serve the `game/` folder as static files, then open the local address in your browser:

```sh
cd game
python3 -m http.server 8000
```

Open `http://localhost:8000/`. Everything is drawn on the canvas, sounds are synthesized in the browser, and the game runs without external assets or network requests.
